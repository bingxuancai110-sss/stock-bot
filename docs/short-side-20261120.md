# Short-side technical research

The short-term page now separates long and short research with a direction switch. The authenticated existing API accepts `direction=long|short` (long remains the default). Sector, pattern, position and pagination filters operate on that direction only. There are no orders, account position changes or LINE pushes.

`short_side.py` evaluates expanding OHLC prefixes independently of the long strategy. Three entries: a confirmed rising support-line break (two closes under the same projected line and buffer), a recent support break (two closes under a prior twenty-session floor and buffer), or a failed rebound into MA20 in a declining MA20/MA60 trend followed by a close under the previous low. Entries require weak MA20 closes, lower-range close, volume ratio at least 1.05, positive target, stop risk at most 6%, and at least 1.5R to the nearest known lower support (or an explicitly labelled 2R reference). ATR/price over 6% or absolute daily move over 9.5% excludes new entries. Institutions annotate only; no price or absolute volume threshold.

Prior-session hard stops take precedence, even when that bar also hits the target; an adverse gap uses the higher open as reference. Moving stops only move down and take effect on the following bar. A single add is 50% of initial units: at least 1R profit, a new ten-session low, volume ratio >=1.25, and combined remaining stop exposure no greater than initial risk. No averaging into losing shorts. Support rejection with MA10 recovery, or two MA20 recovery closes, covers half the current units once. Two closes above prior trend resistance and MA20 with a non-declining MA20 covers all. Hard stops may exit sooner. Every event has the actual levels, indicators, model units and reason. Intraday events remain provisional and cannot replace confirmed position state.

Price difference is initial short price minus cover price; adds and partial covers do not change this deliberately simple per-share metric. It is not account P&L, a verified fill, or net performance: it excludes fees, borrow charges, interest and dividend compensation. Borrow eligibility, regulatory restrictions and available inventory are not verified; this is explicitly labelled in the detail view.

The existing serial worker gets each symbol's quote once and computes both directions from it, checkpointing both into the same atomic JSON snapshot. The full-market queue interleaves an additional bearish technical bucket. No additional network feed, paid service, database table or migration is needed. New namespace `short_term:short-wave-v7` (and `:intraday`) and selection version 12 require a fresh replay; old v6 snapshots remain intact. Ordinary viewing is still read-only. Existing scheduled hours are unchanged. The authenticated manual verification workflow may explicitly request a closing rebuild between 21:00 and midnight on a trading day; the header grants no access by itself and cannot bypass auth or enable a morning/weekend build.
