# Technical observation and entry separation (v8)

Production audit on November 20 showed all 1,979 symbols had been deeply processed: 32 in the 500–999 band and 29 at 1,000+. The long observation list contained none in those two bands. This was not pagination or missing universe coverage. The same-source OHLC audit confirmed that the engine erased high-volatility observations with the same guard used to prohibit an entry. For example, a daily decline over 9.5% was classified as overheated and removed from bearish observation too. A fixed six-percent stop-distance gate, together with the prior-five-day maximum high as the short invalidation for every pattern, rejected otherwise relevant breakdown research with very distant stops. This did not mean those symbols were safe to short immediately.

Changes:

- Close-only preselection and full OHLC observation no longer erase a technically relevant symbol solely because of an extreme session or ATR. The actual entry guard remains: absolute daily move over 9.5% or ATR/price over 6% cannot open a new position. Existing risk actions still run.
- Add bullish trend observation and a separate repair watch when a recent MA20/MA60 uptrend loses MA20. Repair is not a new entry family and cannot independently emit a buy.
- Short invalidation uses the broken floor/rising line (with the current high) or the failed-rebound high as appropriate, plus 0.75 ATR. The five-session-high fallback is retained when no specific pattern exists; a distant fallback is shown as an unmet condition, not silently converted into a trade.
- Both directions use a visible volatility-scaled stop-distance ceiling: `max(6%, min(12%, 2*ATR/close))`. It remains a gate, not a suggested account allocation. At least 1.5R to the reference target, price/volume pattern confirmation and the extreme-session guard still apply. Initial modeled risk budgets continue to limit adds; no losing-position averaging is added.
- Show separate observation, model-position and actual-event badges. A held model with a fresh pattern is no longer mislabeled as waiting for first entry. Untriggered bearish observations no longer assert that a rebound failure has already occurred. An optional MA20 rebound observation level explicitly is not an order.
- Checkpoint high-price full-OHLC diagnostics, including date, bar count, entry/observation flags, actual stop distance, allowed distance, target R and unmet conditions for both directions. Price coverage switches its qualified counts with direction instead of showing long counts under the short tab.

The `short_term:short-wave-v8` namespace and selection version 13 force an independent causal replay; do not relabel v7 historical signals. Previous namespaces remain for rollback. No database schema, holdings, account, LINE or actual trading changes. Existing serial jobs, shared quotes and read-only filters remain.

Validation: synthetic scale-invariance, extreme-session observation without entry, broken-structure versus distant-high stop, repair without buy, numeric deep diagnostics, gap/stop priority, proportional sizing and surrounding regressions. Same-source seven-symbol diagnostic replay retained all seven as bearish observations; it did not claim seven valid new shorts. Raw diagnostic quotes stay untracked and are not fixtures presented as production results. Revert the release to roll back; restart the corresponding older producer to refresh that namespace.
