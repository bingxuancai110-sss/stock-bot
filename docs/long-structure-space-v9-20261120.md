# Long-side structural space (v9)

Continue the v8 observation/admission separation for both directions. The long engine and its proposed-entry preview previously used different overhead targets: the preview could jump to the farthest raw historical high after the original target fell below the proposed entry. This inflated prospective reward. Raw nearby individual highs could also be mistaken for meaningful swing resistance.

`technical_space.long_space` now supplies the shared, entry-relative structural reference. A swing peak needs two completed bars to its right, a five-bar local maximum and prominence of at least max(1 ATR, 0.5% of the proposed entry). Use the nearest confirmed overhead peak, never skip it for a higher R. If no such swing exists, retain the sixty-session range ceiling as a distinct conservative reference. If no known overhead level remains, label the derived target `2R模型參考（非已知壓力）`; it is a model reference, not evidence of a resistance level or assured upside. Only data in the current prefix is used.

Reversal invalidation uses a confirmed pivot low, pullback invalidation uses the local three-session low, and continuation uses its consolidation base. ATR buffers remain below those structures; no intraday future low is used. Pullback/formation widths use bounded ATR allowances instead of only fixed percentage proximity, and a high relative-volume rebound is no longer excluded solely for exceeding 2.5 times volume. Existing extreme-session/ATR admission guards, at-least-1.5R space, volatility-scaled stop ceiling, confirmation, prior hard-stop priority and risk-limited sizing remain in force. Repair is observation only and cannot issue a buy by itself.

The observation level for repair requires MA20 recovery rather than treating a low current price as a buy. Detail shows the proposed level (not an order), invalidation stop, actual first pressure/source, distance percentage and R with a consistent entry basis. Holdings and financial accounts are unchanged.

New namespace `short_term:short-wave-v9` and selection version 14 require a fresh causal replay. v8 and older namespaces stay untouched for rollback. No database migration, extra API or paid service is introduced. Revert this release and refresh the former namespace to roll back. Do not copy old trade events into the new model version.

Validation includes nearest-overhead selection, proposed-entry target changes, confirmed versus unfinished pivots, explicit range/model-reference labels, price-scale invariance and agreement between engine R and displayed structure. Existing tests cover hard-stop/gap priority, add/restore risk budgets, provisional states, high-price watches and per-share spread direction. Browser tests exercise both directions and the surrounding chart/filter interactions. Production verification must distinguish partial saved coverage from a completed whole-market pass.
